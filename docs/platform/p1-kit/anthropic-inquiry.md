Hello,

I am a sole proprietor in Japan and I subscribe to Claude Pro (individual plan). I would like to confirm whether the following use is permitted under the Consumer Terms that apply to users in Japan:

1. I sell small paid services to customers (for example, a personal goal roadmap, a household budget overview, a study plan). I use Claude Pro to create a draft, and I review and edit it myself before delivering it to the customer.
2. Before entering anything into Claude, I remove names and contact details. I only enter the minimum numbers and items needed (for example, monthly study hours or approximate budget categories). Customers consent to this processing in advance.
3. "Help improve Claude" (model training) is turned off. I do not send thumbs-up/down feedback on these conversations.
4. I do not use Claude's output as the basis for investment advice or securities trading.

Questions:
(a) Is this commercial use of Claude Pro permitted for a user in Japan, or should I use a commercial plan (Team) or the API instead?
(b) With model training turned off, how long is the content of these conversations retained?

Thank you.
